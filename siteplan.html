Mide PhoneFix Website Plan

WDD131 W05 Project

1. Site Name

Mide PhoneFix

The name tells visitors exactly what the business does (fixes phones) and where it is located (Ketu, Lagos). It is short, easy to remember, and easy to search for locally.

Optional domain: midephonefix.com

2. Site Purpose

Mide PhoneFix is a phone repair shop with an apprenticeship program. The site lets customers see the repair services offered (screens, batteries, charging ports, software), get price estimates, find the shop location and opening hours, and contact the shop on WhatsApp or by phone. It also gives information about joining the apprenticeship.

3. Scenarios

  • How much does it cost to replace my cracked phone screen, and how long will it take?
  • Where is the shop located, and what time does it open?
  • How can I contact the shop quickly to book a repair?
  • How can I join the phone repair apprenticeship?

  • Montserrat (bold): headings and the site name.
  • Open Sans (regular): body text, paragraphs, lists and forms.
  • Arial, sans-serif: fallback if the web fonts do not load.
